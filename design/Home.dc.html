The workflow

I start with the audience

Content can be accurate and still fail to connect. A piece of content can explain a product, cover every feature, and still leave people wondering why it matters or, worse, miss the target audience entirely.

Years of community management taught me to look for that gap before it shows up. To pay attention to the questions people ask, understand an audience and what they already know, and recognise what they need to hear before a message can resonate.

That's why I believe good content begins before the writing does.

It starts with

  1. Step 1

    Understanding the audience

    Identifying who they are, what they already know, and what they need to know.

  2. Step 2

    Finding the story

    Then I find the story that makes an idea connect with them or feel relevant to them.

  3. Step 3

    Connecting the dots

    From there, I connect the dots: turning research into insight, complex ideas into clear explanations, and brand messages into content people can learn from and connect with.

Before and beyond

I learned to pay attention before I learned to chase attention.

Attention isn't the same as connection.

Before content became my primary focus, I spent years working closely with communities. That experience taught me to listen for the questions people were asking, recognise what they needed to understand, and notice what made them care enough to participate and return.

I bring that same audience-first thinking to content marketing, using what I learn about an audience to shape relevant stories and make complex ideas easier to understand.

Curiosity with structure.

Beyond content, I'm drawn to ideas that need translating, especially in complex, technical, or fast-moving spaces where clarity makes a difference. I use research to understand the subject and the audience, strategy to find what matters, and storytelling to bridge the gap between what brands want to say and what their audiences need to hear.

The goal isn't simply to create or publish more. It's to communicate better.
