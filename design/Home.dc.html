The workflow

I start with the audience

Content can be accurate and still fail to connect. A post can explain a product, a video can cover every feature, and a campaign can fill a calendar, yet leave people wondering why any of it matters. That's why I believe good content begins before the writing does.

It starts with

  1. Step 1

    Understanding the audience

    Identifying who they are and what they need to know.

  2. Step 2

    Finding the story

    Then I find the story that makes an idea relevant to them.

  3. Step 3

    Connecting the dots

    From there, I connect the dots: turning research into insight, complex ideas into clear explanations, and brand messages into content people can learn from and connect with.

Get to know me

Before and beyond

I learned to pay attention before I learned to chase attention.

Attention isn't the same as connection.

Before content became my primary focus, I spent years working closely with communities: understanding their needs, creating opportunities for participation, and learning what makes people return.

Curiosity with structure.

Beyond content, I'm drawn to ideas that need translating, especially in complex, technical, or fast-moving spaces where clarity makes a difference. I use research to understand the subject and the audience, strategy to find what matters, and storytelling to bridge the gap between what brands want to say and what their audiences need to hear.

The goal isn't simply to create or publish more. It's to communicate better.
